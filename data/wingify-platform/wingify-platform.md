## Overview

Wingify Platform is an enterprise experimentation and optimization platform that unifies analytics, experimentation, personalization, feature management, and engagement on a single customer profile. The platform includes six product offerings running on one shared foundation.

## Product Offerings

- **Experimentation**: Website experimentation built for non-technical teams
- **Personalization**: Real-time adaptive personalization
- **Analyze**: Heatmaps, session recordings, funnels, and surveys
- **Feature Management**: Feature flagging, rollouts, and server-side experimentation
- **Commerce**: Semantic search, product & content recommendations, and merchandising
- **Engage**: Behavior-triggered browser push notifications

## Deployment

Cloud deployment with data centers in the US, the EU, and India. Website experiments deploy via a SmartCode snippet; feature management requires SDK integration into the codebase.

## Integrations

Wingify integrates with tools across the marketing and product stack. Full integration details are available at wingify.com/integrations/.
